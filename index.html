4. Learn the Science

Weighted input: signal × connection weight.

Threshold: the minimum total needed for the neuron to output 1.

Binary output: output is 1 if the total is greater than or equal to the threshold; otherwise, output is 0.

This is a simplified artificial neuron model, not a full simulation of biological neurons or real brain activity.
